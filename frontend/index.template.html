Provenance

Every rupee originates in bookings.net_room_revenue or bookings.distribution_cost, computed by PostgreSQL from GENERATED ALWAYS AS ... STORED columns over IMMUTABLE functions. This page formats and sorts those values; it does not compute revenue. The Mix Simulator is the one exception and replays ScenarioEngine.evaluate against the same observed rates, an agreement enforced to one paisa by the test suite.

On the words used here

Net revenue is not profit. It is gross room revenue after discount, commission, payment fee and other channel cost. This dataset models no labour, utilities or overhead, so no margin or profit figure is claimed anywhere. Figures labelled Modelled depend on assumptions the hotel supplies and are stated as ceilings or illustrations — never as guaranteed savings.
